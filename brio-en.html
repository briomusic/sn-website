Gestalt coaching


“What is, may be. And what may be, can change.”
(loosely after Arnold Beisser)

Change often doesn't come from wanting to be different – but from first noticing how things actually are right now. Whoever stops fighting against themselves gains space. And in that space, something can begin to move.

Where Gestalt coaching can help

  • upheavals and transitions – at work, in relationships, between phases of life
  • decisions where head and gut are saying different things
  • the feeling of being stuck, or of finding oneself in the same pattern again and again
  • feelings that are hard to make sense of – or the sense of feeling very little
  • the question of how to stay capable of acting and connected in uncertain times
My Gestalt coaching is counselling, not psychotherapy. It is not intended for acute crises or as a substitute for treatment. In an introductory meeting, we find out together whether it is a good fit.

How we work together

  • exploring together instead of handing out ready-made advice
  • noticing how body and feelings respond to what we experience and think
  • looking at the many sides of being human with mindfulness and humour – including the uncomfortable ones
  • nobody has a monopoly on the truth – we set out together and let ourselves be surprised by what shows up
  • working on change and acceptance – and on change through acceptance

About me

For many years I worked as a musician and producer. Today, working with people is at the centre of what I do.

From music, I bring the experience that what matters most often lies in listening and improvising – in the courage not to always know where things are headed.

What moves me is emotional agency at a time when some certainties are falling away – socially, ecologically, and in the roles we have grown into. What helps us stay connected and flexible?

Training: basic Gestalt training (Irgendwie Anders e.V.) · advanced Gestalt training (Rebecca Stadler) · preparing for the Heilpraktiker examination in psychotherapy (Paracelsus Gesundheitsakademie)
