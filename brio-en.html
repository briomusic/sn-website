About me


I am currently completing advanced training in Gestalt therapy with Rebecca Stadler and preparing for the Heilpraktiker for psychotherapy examination at the Paracelsus Gesundheitsakademie. Before that, I completed a basic Gestalt training at Irgendwie Anders e.V.

What moves me is emotional agency at a time when certainties are falling away – socially, ecologically, and in the roles we have grown into. What helps us stay connected and awake?

My approach

"What is, may be. And what may be, can change."
(loosely after Arnold Beisser)

I am interested in how body and soul respond to what we experience and think – and what shows up when we look more closely. This is why I find the Gestalt approach particularly appealing:
  • exploring together instead of handing out ready-made advice
  • a mindful, yet humorous way of relating to the many aspects of the human experience
  • the awareness that nobody has the fountain of truth – we set out together and let ourselves be surprised by what we find
  • working on change and acceptance at the same time – and on change through acceptance
From many years as a musician, I bring the experience that what matters most often lies in listening and improvising – in the courage not to always know where things are headed.

Coaching offer


During my Gestalt training, I am offering a few places for Gestalt coaching on a pro bono basis, i.e. without a fee. I only ask for a contribution towards the room rent. Sessions last 60 minutes and take place in Prädikow (room: €10) or Berlin (room: €20).
My work is accompanied by regular supervision and intervision.

This is counselling, not psychotherapy. The offer is not intended for acute crises or as a substitute for psychotherapy. If you are interested, feel free to get in touch for a no-obligation introductory meeting.
